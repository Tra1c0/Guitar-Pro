ArtiFret Pearl Kit
=================

The drum sounds of this app (file assets/banks/artifret-pearl-kit.rwdk) are a
modified version of the sample library "AVL Black Pearl 4" from the AVL-Drumkits.

Original sample library
  AVL-Drumkits, Black Pearl 4
  Created by: Glen MacArthur 2015
  Website: http://www.bandshed.net/avldrumkits/
  Contact: info@bandshed.net
  Source used: Black_Pearl_4_LV2.sf2 from https://github.com/x42/avldrums.lv2
  (commit 9389f16f139410d9a55f9006976bd54cbd8b7806,
  SHA-256 e0542ec19f2816ef4e0d00aeb087e7aa311e888d6f4805f971688bb267c6d405)
  The complete readme of the original is included unchanged as
  licenses/avl-drumkits-README.txt.

Modifications (tools/make_drum_bank.py)
  - converted from SoundFont 2 to the app's own drum-kit format (RWDK),
  - General MIDI drum notes mapped onto the kit; some toms are played transposed,
    the China cymbal plays crash 3, rimshots play the recorded edge hits,
  - leading near-silence and the silent end of each sample removed, short fades
    added, cymbal samples shortened to at most 6 seconds,
  - samples not used by the mapping (crash/ride chokes, hi-hat swish) left out.
  Sample rate (44.1 kHz) and resolution (16 bit) are unchanged.
